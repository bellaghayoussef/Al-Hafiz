# Privacy Policy for Al-Hafiz

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2026  
**Application Name:** Al-Hafiz (الْحَافِظُ)  
**Package Name:** `com.alhafiz.quran.athan`  

---

### 1. Introduction
Al-Hafiz ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the **Al-Hafiz** mobile application.

Al-Hafiz is a 100% free, ad-free Islamic application providing Quran reading, per-ayah audio recitation, Athan prayer time calculation, and spaced repetition memorization tracking (Hifz). **We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.**

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use

#### A. Location Information
- **Purpose:** Al-Hafiz requests permission to access your device's geographical location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) solely to calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and determine Qibla direction.
- **Data Handling:** All location calculation is performed **100% locally on your device**. Your location coordinates are **never transmitted** to external servers, third parties, or saved permanently.

#### B. Notifications and Alarm Permissions
- **Purpose:** The app requests `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, and `USE_EXACT_ALARM` permissions to deliver local Athan prayer call alerts and daily memorization review reminders.
- **Data Handling:** Notifications are generated and scheduled strictly on your local device. We do not use remote push notification servers or track when notifications are received.

#### C. Local Storage
- **Stored Data:** Your memorization progress (Hifz entries), bookmarked verses, selected reciter preferences, and theme settings are saved **locally on your device** using local Hive storage.
- **Data Control:** You have full control over your data. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data completely removes all stored preferences from your device.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Al-Hafiz does **NOT** contain:
- ❌ Third-party advertising networks (No AdMob, Meta Ads, or Unity Ads).
- ❌ Third-party analytics tools (No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
- ❌ User tracking scripts or commercial cookies.

Audio recitations and Quranic texts are fetched directly via public open-source Islamic APIs (AlQuran Cloud API, MP3Quran, and EveryAyah). These connections are standard HTTPS requests used exclusively to retrieve requested audio files and text.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Al-Hafiz is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone.

---

### 5. Security
Because all your data remains exclusively on your local device, your information is protected by your device's built-in operating system security measures.
